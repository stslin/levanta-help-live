# Levanta Account Setup Guide: Amazon, Walmart, Shopify & Brand Configuration

Last updated 10 days ago

This guide walks you through the core steps to set up and manage your Levanta account — from connecting your Amazon account to configuring your brand profile and managing your marketplace connections. Follow each section to ensure your account is fully configured and ready to run.

## Step 1: Connect Your Amazon Account

To get started, you'll need to connect your Amazon account to Levanta. This enables attribution tracking, product syncing, and sample fulfillment.

  1. Navigate to **Settings → Connections**

  2. Under the **Amazon** tab, click **\+ Add Amazon Connection**

  3. Follow the prompts to authorize your Amazon Ads account

Once connected, your associated brands and products will be pulled in automatically.

**There are two types of Amazon connections:**

  * **Amazon Ads** — Required for Amazon Attribution tracking. This syncs your brand and products so clicks and conversions are tracked correctly in Levanta

  * **Amazon Selling Partner** — Required if you plan to send product samples to creators through the platform. Once connected, you'll see a **Samples Enabled** badge on the connection.

> ⚠ __**_Note:_**_Multiple Amazon accounts are supported. Repeat the steps above to add additional accounts if needed._

## Step 2: Connect Walmart

If you sell on Walmart, you can connect your Walmart account to recruit creators, set custom commissions, and access Walmart-specific reporting — all from the same Levanta dashboard.

  1. Navigate to **Settings → Connections**

  2. Click the **Walmart** tab

  3. Click **\+ Add Walmart Connection** and follow the prompts

> ⚠ __**_Note:_**_Once your Walmart connection is added, notify your CSM so they can complete the activation on the backend._

## Step 3: Connect Shopify

Sellers with a Shopify store can connect it to Levanta to track DTC sales and expand their affiliate program beyond Amazon.

  1. Navigate to **Settings → Connections**

  2. Click the **Shopify** tab

  3. Click **\+ Add Shopify Connection** and follow the authorization prompts

## Step 4: Set Up Your Brand Profile

Your Brand Profile is visible to creators in the Levanta marketplace. A complete, well-presented profile builds trust and increases your acceptance rate among quality creators.

Navigate to [**Brands**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/6655213348-your-levanta-onboarding-checklist-a-step-by-step-setup-guide-for-sellers) and select your brand to access the Brand Profile editor. Fill in the following:

  * **Brand Bio** — A short description of your brand and what you sell

  * **Amazon Storefront URL** — Links creators directly to your Amazon storefront

  * **Contact Email** — The email address creators can use to reach your team

  * **Terms and Conditions** — Link to your brand's affiliate terms

### **Troubleshooting: Amazon Storefront URL Not Pulling In ✦ NEW**

If your Amazon storefront URL isn't appearing correctly on your Levanta brand profile, or creators are reporting that the storefront link isn't working, the issue is almost always on the Amazon side. _Here's what to check:_

### **How to Create Your Amazon Brand Store**

If you don't yet have an Amazon Brand Store, here's how to get one set up:

  1. **Register for Amazon Brand Registry** at [brandservices.amazon.com](http://brandservices.amazon.com). You'll need an active registered trademark for your brand.

  2. **Sign in to your Amazon Ads advertising console** and click **Brand Store**.

  3. Use Amazon's **self-service Store builder** to design your storefront. You can use pre-built templates and a drag-and-drop editor to add products, imagery, and brand content.

  4. **Submit your store for review** when you're ready. Moderation takes up to 24 hours.

  5. Once approved and live, **copy your storefront URL** and paste it into your Levanta brand profile under **Amazon Storefront URL**.

## Step 5: Upload Brand Assets

Levanta now includes a dedicated **Manage Brand Assets** feature that lets you upload and manage your brand logos directly within the platform. These assets are used across Levanta's creator-facing discovery pages, featured brand placements, and branded creator sign-up flows.

To access it, navigate to your **Brand Profile** and click the **Manage Brand Assets** button in the top right corner of the Brand Profile card. This opens an asset management drawer where you can upload:

  * **Logo file (colored, for white background)** — Used on light backgrounds across creator-facing pages. Upload as a square SVG or PNG (max 2MB)

  * **Logo file (white, for dark background)** — Used on dark backgrounds. PNG with transparency is preferred. Upload as a square SVG or PNG (max 2MB)

  * **Lifestyle watermark file** — Appears as a watermark behind your white logo on creator discovery pages. Upload as SVG or PNG (max 2MB)

Once uploaded, click **Preview** to review how your assets appear, or use **Copy to other Brands** to apply the same assets across all your brands at once — a major time-saver if you manage multiple brands under one account.

>
> ⚠ __**_Note:_**_Brand assets are used to power featured brand placements in Levanta's creator discovery experience and branded creator sign-up flows. Keeping these up to date improves your brand's visibility in the marketplace._

## Step 6: Manage Multiple Marketplaces

Levanta supports managing your affiliate program across multiple marketplaces — including Amazon (global regions), Walmart, and Shopify — all from a single account.

Use the marketplace selector to toggle between regions and retailers. Your **Payments** section provides a unified view of all invoices across all connected marketplaces, regardless of which marketplace is currently selected.

If you'd like to see invoices for just one marketplace, you don't need to leave the unified view, simply use the **_Filters_** button in Payments and select the marketplace you want from the Marketplace dropdown. This narrows the invoice list down to that marketplace without affecting what's shown elsewhere in Levanta.

**Key Notes:**

  * Each Levanta module—Dashboard, Products, Brands, etc. will display data based on the currently selected marketplace.

  * The Payments module is an exception and will show a unified view of all invoices across marketplaces, regardless of which marketplace is selected in the toggle. Use the Filters button within Payments if you'd like to narrow that view down to a single marketplace instead.

  * Depending on your current plan, you may need to upgrade to access additional global Amazon regions or Walmart functionality.

# Your Levanta Onboarding Checklist: A Step-by-Step Setup Guide for Sellers

Last updated 16 days ago

## Getting Started on Levanta: Complete Seller Onboarding Guide

This guide walks you through every step of setting up and launching your affiliate program on Levanta — from creating your account to recruiting creators, running campaigns, and optimizing performance. Follow each phase in order to ensure a smooth and successful launch.

## Phase 1: Account Registration & Team Setup

Before doing anything else, get your account and billing in place.

  * **Sign up** for a Levanta account at [app.levanta.io](http://app.levanta.io)

  * **Add a payment method** via Stripe under your account billing settings

  * **Choose your preferred payment method type** (credit card, bank transfer, etc.)

  * **Enable Auto-Billing** _(optional but recommended)_ to avoid any interruptions to your program

## Phase 2: Amazon, Walmart & Shopify Integration

##  _Amazon_

Connecting your Amazon accounts is essential for tracking sales and enabling key platform features.

  * **Connect your Amazon Ads account** to enable Amazon Attribution tracking — this is required for clicks and conversions to appear in Levanta

  * **Add your Amazon Selling Partner (SP) connection** if you plan to use product sampling _(Seller Central → Settings → Connections → last option)_

> ⚠ __**_Note:_**_The Amazon Selling Partner connection is required to send product samples through Levanta._

## **_Walmart_**

  * Navigate to **Settings → Connections → Walmart** tab

  * Click **\+ Add Walmart Connection** and follow the authorization prompts to connect your Walmart Marketplace or Supplier account

  * If you're on a launch plan or need backend activation, notify your CSM or reach out to [cs@levanta.io](mailto:cs@levanta.io) to complete setup

> ⚠ __**_Note:_**_Connecting Walmart lets you view Walmart performance alongside Amazon and Shopify in one dashboard._

## _Shopify_

  * Navigate to **Settings → Connections → Shopify** tab

  * Click **+**[**Add Shopify Connection**](https://apps.shopify.com/levanta) — this redirects you to the Levanta app in the Shopify App Store

  * Log in to your Shopify account and click **Install** to complete the connection

## Phase 3: Brand Profile & Asset Setup

Your brand profile is the first thing creators see when they discover your program. A complete, well-presented profile builds trust and increases your acceptance rate among quality creators.

**Complete your Brand Page** by filling in the following — all of this is visible to creators in the Levanta marketplace:

  * Brand bio

  * Contact details

  * Amazon Storefront URL

  * Email

  * Terms and conditions

**Upload your Brand Assets** using the new **Manage Brand Assets** feature. Click the **Manage Brand Assets** button on your Brand Profile card to open the asset management drawer. Here you can upload:

  * **Logo file (colored, for white background)** — Used across Levanta's creator-facing pages and discovery experiences. Upload as a square SVG or PNG (max 2MB)

  * **Logo file (white, for dark background)** — Used on dark backgrounds. PNG with transparency is preferred. Upload as a square SVG or PNG (max 2MB)

  * **Lifestyle watermark file** — An SVG or PNG that appears as a watermark behind your white logo on creator discovery pages

Once uploaded, use the **Copy to other Brands** option to apply the same assets across multiple brands instantly — a major time-saver if you manage several brands under one account.

> ⚠ __**_Note:_**_Brand assets are used to power featured brand placements in Levanta's creator discovery experience and branded creator sign-up flows. Keeping these up to date improves your brand's visibility in the marketplace._

## Phase 4: Product Catalog & Commission Setup

Activate your brand and configure the commission structure that will attract the right creators.

  1. [**Activate your brand** ](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1167493894-activating-your-brand-and-setting-affiliate-terms)— Navigate to **Brands → select your brand → Activate** , review the terms, and acknowledge the geo notice

  2. **Enable auto-accept** for creator applications _(recommended)_ — this removes friction and helps you grow your creator base faster

  3. **Set your public commission rate** — the Levanta platform average is ~20%

  4. **Set a private commission rate** for high-value creators — the platform average is ~25%

  5. **Activate your products** so creators can generate attribution links and start promoting

## Phase 5: Creator Recruitment & Social Discovery

With your program ready, it's time to build your creator network.

  * **Browse the Creator Directory** to discover creators relevant to your niche and audience

  * **Send bulk partnership invites** — go to **Directory → select creators → Bulk Actions → Send Partnership Invites**. Always include a personalized message to improve your acceptance rate

  * **Set up Creator Groups** to segment your partners by type (e.g., deal sites, publishers, social influencers) for easier management and targeted outreach

  * [**Share your Branded Creator Sign-Up Page**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/7672914299-getting-started-with-creators#branded-creator-sign-up-page) — find it under **Creators → Applications → Branded Creator Sign-Up Page**. Share this link so creators can join your program directly and quickly

  * Use **_Social Discovery_** to find and recruit creators directly from **_TikTok and Meta (Instagram/Facebook)_** , even if they're not yet on Levanta — go to **Creators → Social Discovery**

> ⚠ __**_Note:_**__Social Discovery_ surfaces creators beyond the standard Creator Marketplace, which only shows creators already on Levanta._

## Phase 6: Creator Activation & Communication

Getting creators approved is just the start — activation comes from consistent, timely communication.

  * **Review and approve creator applications daily** if auto-accept is not enabled

  * [**Send a welcome message**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/5677175981-communicating-with-creators-messaging-tool-vs-brand-announcements) to every newly approved creator via the Messaging tool. Include your top products, commission rate, attribution window, media kit link, and a point of contact

  * **Publish Brand Announcements** to keep all partnered creators informed — navigate to **Brands → Announcements → Create Announcement** _(max 2 per 7 days)_

  * **Export your active creator list** for off-platform email outreach if needed — go to **Creators → Active → Export CSV**. The export includes creator email addresses

  * **Review and set private commissions** for your highest-value creators to give them an added incentive to prioritize your brand

## Phase 7: Campaigns & Promotions

Drive additional performance through strategic campaigns and placements.

  1. [**Deals**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/5961962884-how-to-create-deals) Upload deals ahead of upcoming promotions by navigating to **Brands → Deals → Create Deal**. Schedule deals 4–6 weeks before peak seasons. Note that creators are not automatically notified when a deal is published — pair deal uploads with an Announcement or direct message.

  2. **CPC Campaigns** Consider launching a Cost-Per-Click (CPC) campaign, especially when entering new product categories, to drive early traffic and visibility.

  3. **Creator Connections (CC) Integration** Connect your Creator Connections integration to unlock Levanta's CC-specific features _(refer to your CSM for current setup steps)_. Once connected:

  * Create your first CC campaign in Levanta via **CC tab → Campaigns → Create**

  * Set up the **Loyalty Bonus Program (LBP)** to reward top CC creators with custom bonus tiers — navigate to **CC tab → Loyalty Bonus**. Bonuses are tracked and billed on your monthly invoice

** _Paid Placements_** \- Consider Paid Placements to secure guaranteed content from top creators:

  * Browse creator Rate Cards and send a placement proposal

  * Negotiate the price and finalize terms

  * A contract auto-generates and payment is held in escrow until content is submitted

⚠ __**_Important:_**_For any Paid Placement deal, always ask the creator to include your Levanta attribution link in their content — this ensures the placement also drives tracked sales conversions._

**_Learn more about our Paid Placement feature_**[** _here_**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1721345900-getting-started-with-paid-placements-for-sellers)** _._**

## Phase 8: Additional Channels _(Optional)_

Expand your program beyond Amazon by connecting additional retail channels.

**Walmart**

  * Navigate to **Settings → Connections → Add Walmart** to connect your Walmart account

  * Notify your CSM once connected so they can complete the activation on the backend

  * Set Walmart-specific commissions and recruit creators who are compatible with Walmart promotions

**Shopify**

  * Navigate to **Settings → Connections → Add Shopify** to connect your Shopify store

  * Once connected, activate your Shopify products and set commissions for DTC sales tracking

## Phase 9: Reporting & Ongoing Optimization

A strong program requires consistent monitoring and proactive management. Review these reports regularly to stay on top of performance.

Report| Where to Find It| What to Look For
---|---|---
**Performance Dashboard**|  Reports → Performance| Overall clicks, conversions, and sales by creator
**Creator Report**|  Reports → Creator| Top performers to consider for private commissions or LBP upgrades
**New to Brand (NTB) Report**|  Reports → More Reports → New to Brand| First-time customer acquisition rate
**BSR Report**|  Reports → More Reports → BSR Report| Organic rank lift driven by affiliate-generated conversions
**BRB Report**|  Reports → More Reports → BRB Report| Estimated Brand Referral Bonus earnings
**CC Campaign Report**|  CC tab → Campaigns| Creator Connections performance alongside standard Levanta data

**Ongoing best practices:**

  * **Re-engage inactive creators** with a personalized message, a private commission offer, or a Paid Placement proposal

  * **Brief creators before every peak season** — upload relevant Deals, publish an Announcement, then follow up via Messenger with your top partners for maximum impact

# What is Levanta for Shopify?

Last updated 16 days ago

**Levanta for Shopify** is a native integration that enables brands to manage affiliate and creator partnerships for their Shopify storefront within the Levanta platform, alongside existing Amazon and Walmart programs.

Scale affiliate and creator programs across Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify from one unified system.

## **Who is this for?**

  * Brands selling across Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify that want one platform to manage creator partnerships, performance, and payouts.

  * Direct-to-Consumer brands that are seeking a modern alternative to legacy affiliate platforms with a creator-first, performance-driven approach.

## **Why Shopify?**

Shopify represents the center of gravity for Direct-to-Consumer commerce, with millions of active stores. However, affiliate tools in the Shopify ecosystem remain fragmented, outdated, and difficult to manage.

Levanta’s Shopify integration enables brands to:

  * Take back margin and control

  * Reduce operational complexity

  * Run one creator program across all sales channels

  * Replace siloed, legacy affiliate tools with a modern platform

## **Problems Levanta for Shopify Solves**

### **Enterprise / Omnichannel Challenges**

  * **Cost pressure:** Leaner teams and shrinking margins demand efficient, performance-driven marketing.

  * **Disjointed programs:** Brands rely on multiple tools across Amazon, Walmart, and DTC.

  * **Outdated affiliate platforms:** Legacy tools are built for traditional affiliate marketing, not modern creator partnerships.

  * **Limited performance visibility:** Hard to measure incremental revenue and brand halo across channels.

### **Single-Channel (Shopify-Focused) Challenges**

  * Creator marketing is expensive and time-consuming to start.

  * Recruiting, onboarding, and tracking partners are manual processes.

  * Traditional affiliate tools feel clunky and outdated.

  * Limited clarity into what partnerships actually drive results.

##
**Key Benefits**

### **Run One Unified Creator Program**

Manage creators and affiliates across Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify in one platform. This reduces cost and operational overhead.

### **Activate Creators Faster**

Use AI-powered discovery, social listening, and product seeding tools to recruit and launch partnerships quickly.

### **Optimize Spend with Cross-Channel Insights**

Measure creator performance and incremental impact across marketplaces and DTC in real time.

### **Simplify Operations**

Set commissions, send samples, track performance, and manage payouts in one place.

### **Gain Clear Performance Visibility**

See ROI, content performance, and payouts across channels without manual reporting.

## **Core Platform Capabilities**

### **Discovery & Recruitment**

Find high-performing creators through Levanta’s AI-powered Creator Marketplace.

### **Product Samples & Gifting**

Automate product seeding from request through delivery. To learn more, please check our guide on [_Managing Product Samples in Levanta_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/8755198258-how-to-send-samples-to-creators).

### **Paid Placements**

Negotiate and manage flat-fee collaborations in one platform. Please check our guide on [_Getting Started with Paid Placements (For Sellers)_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1721345900-getting-started-with-paid-placements-for-sellers) to learn more about this feature.

### **Social Listening**

Identify creators already talking about your brand or products.

###
**Reporting & Analytics**

Track affiliate and creator performance, Brand reports, and Product reports across Shopify, Amazon, and Walmart in real time.

### **Payments & Taxes**

Send one payment. Levanta distributes funds and handles tax documentation.

### **Native Integrations**

One-click integrations with Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify.

## **How to Set Up Your Levanta Account with Shopify Marketplace**

Follow the steps below to connect your Shopify store to Levanta and start managing your brands and products:

### **Go to Settings → Connections**

  * Navigate to **Settings** , then open **Connections**.

  * You’ll see a new tab labeled **Shopify**.

### **Add your Shopify connection**

  * Click **Add Shopify Connection**.

  * This will redirect you to the Levanta app within the Shopify App Store.

### **Install the Levanta app**

  * Log in to your Shopify account.

  * Click Install to complete the connection.

### **Activate your brand**

  * After installation, activate your brand inside Levanta.

  * For detailed steps, read our guide on [Managing Brands and Products in Levanta](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1167493894-activating-your-brand-and-setting-affiliate-terms).

## **Syncing Amazon & Walmart Partnerships to Shopify**

Sellers can now seamlessly sync their existing Amazon and Walmart partnerships directly to Shopify.

To enable this feature:

  1. Navigate to your **Brand Detail Page**.

  2. Click the **Settings** wheel.

  3. Select **Sync Partnerships**.

This allows you to extend your existing Amazon and Walmart creator partnerships to your Shopify storefront, helping you maintain continuity across channels and maximize creator performance without rebuilding partnerships from scratch.

We recommend reviewing your synced partnerships to ensure commission structures and terms align with your Shopify strategy.

## **Summary**

Levanta for Shopify brings Direct-to-Consumer creator marketing into the same ecosystem as Amazon and Walmart, enabling your brands to:

  * Consolidate tools and reduce costs

  * Scale creator partnerships faster

  * Measure performance across all channels

  * Drive incremental revenue and brand growth

This integration marks a major step toward a unified, performance-driven creator economy for modern ecommerce brands.

# Getting Started with Creators

Last updated 16 days ago

## **Collaborating with Creators on Levanta**

The **Creator Marketplace** makes it easy to discover, filter, and connect with creators directly in your Levanta dashboard.

### **Exploring the Marketplace**

  * Navigate to **Creators → Discover**.

  * You will immediately see **Featured Creators** , which highlight Levanta’s top-performing partners. To view the full list, click **“See All Featured.”**

  * Scroll to browse creators by categories such as:

**_Social Influencers_**

** _Deal Sites_**

** _Publishers_**

** _Loyalty Platforms_**

** _Media Buyers_**

** _Affiliate Networks_**

These categories help you easily find partners that best align with your brand and campaign goals.

### **Viewing All Creators**

  * Select **See More** to explore the full marketplace.

### **Creator Directory**

The **Creator Directory** helps you easily discover, filter, and connect with creators that are relevant to your brand.

From this page, you can:

  * **Search creators** using the search bar.

  * **Filter creators** based on specific criteria such as:

  1. _CC Status_

  2.  _Product Categories_

  3.  _Promotional Methods_

  4.  _Platforms_

  5.  _Activity_

  6.  _TikTok Followers_

  7.  _Partnership Status_

  8.  _Creator Groups_

  * Use Bulk Actions to send partnership invitations to multiple creators at once.

  * Use the **Recommended** dropdown to sort creators in the directory. You can view **Recommended creators** suggested for your brand or switch to **Recently Joined** to discover the newest creators who have recently joined the platform.

These tools help you quickly find and connect with creators who best match your campaign goals and audience.

### **Inviting Creators**

  * Click **Send Invitation** on the right-hand side of a creator’s profile

  * For better results, include a **personalized message** with your invite.

While the Creator Marketplace is a powerful tool for quick wins in affiliate recruitment, the **most successful programs also recruit outside of Levanta**. Combine marketplace outreach with your own recruitment strategies to maximize growth.

# Creator Groups: Organize, Segment & Manage Your Creator Partners

Last updated 16 days ago

## How and why to use Creator Groups

Creator Groups is a powerful feature in Levanta that lets you organize your creator partners into logical segments for more efficient management, communication, and incentive management. Whether you're grouping creators by niche, performance tier, traffic source, or content type, Groups help you take targeted action at scale — without managing every creator relationship individually.

## How to Access Creator Groups

Navigate to **Creators → Groups** in the left navigation menu to access your Creator Groups dashboard. From here you can view all existing groups, see member counts, and manage or delete groups as needed.

## How to Create a Group

  1. Navigate to **Creators → Groups**

  2. Click **Create Group** in the top right corner

  3. Enter a **Group Name** — choose something descriptive that reflects the segment (e.g., "Top Performers," "Deal Sites," "Holiday Partners")

  4. Add a **Description** to help your team understand what the group is for

  5. Click **Create New Group**

## How to Add Creators to a Group

Levanta offers three ways to add creators to a group depending on where you are in the platform:

**From the Groups Page** Open any existing group and click **Quick Add Members** to search for and add creators directly from the group's dedicated page.

**From the Group Manager Widget** The Group Manager Widget is available throughout the platform — including the Creator discovery view, the Creator catalog list view, and individual Creator detail pages. Click the widget on any creator card to assign them to an existing group or create a new one on the spot. Groups with a green checkmark indicate the creator is already a member.

**From the Creator Directory** Browse the Directory, select multiple creators, and use **Bulk Actions** to add them to a group all at once — ideal when building a new segment from scratch.

## How to Remove a Creator from a Group

  1. Navigate to **Creators → Groups**

  2. Click on the **Group Name** to open the group's dedicated page

  3. Locate the creator you want to remove

  4. Under the **Actions** column, click the **Remove Member** icon (red button) next to the creator's name

The creator will be removed from the group immediately. This does not affect their partnership status or any active commissions — it only removes them from that specific group.

## Tips for Using Creator Groups Effectively

  * **Segment by type** — Create separate groups for deal sites, publishers, social influencers, and niche content creators to tailor outreach and commissions for each

  * **Use groups for private commissions** — Instead of setting rates creator by creator, assign your top tier to a group and apply a group-wide private commission in one action

  * **Build seasonal groups** — Before major sales events, create a dedicated group for your most active partners so you can reach them quickly with exclusive offers

  * **Keep descriptions clear** — If you manage a team, descriptive group names and descriptions help everyone understand the purpose of each segment at a glance

# Understanding Different Types of Creators in Levanta

Last updated 16 days ago

### **Build a high-performing creator strategy with diverse partnerships**

To drive incremental sales and expand your brand's visibility, work with a diverse mix of creator types. Each serves a different role in the customer journey, from discovery to conversion, and a well-rounded strategy ensures you're reaching shoppers at every stage of the funnel.

At Levanta, every creator is vetted to ensure quality, credibility, and alignment with platform standards. Below is an overview of the creator types you'll encounter and how each can benefit your brand.

### _📸 Social Influencer (Content Creator)_

Creators who build engaged audiences on social platforms, including bloggers and YouTubers. They promote your brand through personal, authentic content across Instagram, Facebook, YouTube, TikTok, and more.

  * Showcase products with authenticity and broad reach

  * Build social proof and trust through personal recommendations

  * Drive discovery among audiences who aren't actively searching for your product

###  _📰 Publisher (Editorial Media)_

Editorial sites or media outlets with journalistic standards, not social-first creators. These partners produce in-depth reviews, comparisons, roundups, and how-to guides with editorial independence.

  * Leverage SEO, newsletters, and loyal readership for sustained visibility

  * Deliver high-converting, evergreen content backed by thoroughly vetted reviews

  * Build long-term brand credibility through trusted third-party validation

###  _🔗 Affiliate Network_

Companies that connect brands with multiple affiliates and platforms, enabling product discovery and monetization across content and shopping sites.

  * Improve conversion tracking and attribution

  * Expand your reach across a network of partners through a single relationship

  * Unlock tools that make it easier for creators to feature your products

###  _🎯 Media Buyer (Paid Traffic)_

Marketers who drive traffic by purchasing ad space — Paid Search, Facebook Ads, and more — to promote your products at scale.

  * Appear in high-converting ad placements

  * Generate large traffic volumes with measurable, often immediate ROI

  * May request higher commission rates tied to performance

###  _🏷 Deal Site (Deal and Coupon Aggregators)_

Coupon and deal platforms; not social media accounts. These partners surface your products to price-conscious shoppers actively seeking discounts.

  * Drive first-time purchases and discovery among deal-seekers

  * Boost sales during seasonal events, promotions, or flash sales

  * Capture high-intent shoppers who are ready to buy

###  _💳 Loyalty Platform (Cash-Back/Rewards)_

Operators of cash-back, points, or loyalty programs where users earn rewards for purchases.

  * Drive repeat purchases and long-term customer retention

  * Encourage larger basket sizes through reward incentives

  * Appeal to value-focused audiences already in buying mode, often through dedicated apps and browser extensions

## Build a Balanced Creator Mix

No single creator type will maximize your results alone. The strongest strategies layer multiple types together:

  * **Social Influencers** and **Publishers** introduce your brand to new audiences

  * **Affiliate Networks** extend your reach across ecosystems

  * **Media Buyers** , **Deal Sites** , and **Loyalty Platforms** drive discovery _and_ convert that awareness into sales

Diversifying your partnerships protects against over-reliance on any one channel and helps you capture shoppers wherever they are in their journey.

## Overview

Levanta offers two primary tools for communicating with your creators: **In-App Messaging** and **Brand Announcements**. While both help you stay connected with your partner network, each serves a distinct purpose in your creator management strategy. Knowing when to use each one ensures your messages reach the right creators in the right way.

## 1\. Brand Announcements

### Recommended for Broad Updates

Brand Announcements is the go-to tool for keeping your partnered creators informed about brand updates. It provides a centralized communication experience and reduces inbox clutter for creators — so your most important updates always get the visibility they deserve.

**When to use Brand Announcements:**

  * **New Product Launches** — Highlight new additions to your catalog and get creators promoting them quickly

  * **Seasonal Promotions** — Announce Black Friday, Cyber Monday, Prime Day deals, or other time-sensitive campaigns

  * **Brand News** — Share media kits, brand updates, or new partnership opportunities with your full creator base

**Key benefits:**

  * **Visibility** — Announcements appear on your brand page, the creator's dedicated Announcements tab, and in their daily summary emails — making them hard to miss

  * **Rich Content** — Tag specific products and attach files directly to your announcement for a more informative, actionable update

  * **Efficiency** — Eliminates the need for repetitive one-off messages when communicating broad updates to all partners

## How to Create an Announcement

Navigate to **Brands → Announcements** and follow the steps below:

  1. Click **Create new announcement**

  2. Select the **Brand** you want to send the announcement from

  3. Choose your **Recipients:**

     * All partnered creators

     * A specific creator group

     * All partnered creators except specific creators

  4. Enter your announcement details and message

  5. Click **Publish** to send

> ** _Note:_**_You can send up to_** _2 announcements every 7 days_** _per brand._

## 2\. Messaging Tool

### Best for Targeted & Direct Outreach

The Messaging tool is best suited for direct, two-way communication and personalized outreach. Use it when the conversation needs to feel intentional and individual rather than broadcast.

**When to use the Messaging tool:**

  * **Personalized Onboarding** — Send individual "Welcome" messages to newly approved creators with specific action items to get them started

  * **Private Offers** — Share unique, one-off promo codes or incentives that shouldn't be visible to your entire partner list

  * **Relationship Building** — Have 1-on-1 conversations to discuss content strategies, sample requests, or performance feedback

  * **Targeted Group Outreach** — Message a specific Creator Group (e.g., your "Top Performers" or "Tech Reviewers") for more focused, relevant communication

**Best practices:**

  * **Use Templates** — Message templates help maintain consistency across outreach while still allowing for quick personalization

  * **Check Your Persona Settings** — Before sending, confirm you're messaging from the correct persona (Brand View vs. Seller View) so creators see the right identity associated with your message.

Feature| Best For| Visibility
---|---|---

**Messaging Tool**|  Direct outreach: welcome notes, 1-on-1 support, and private offers| Creator inbox / conversation thread

## Cost-Per-Click Best Practices, Product Demo, and FAQ

**CPC Overview and Benefits**

Cost-Per-Click (CPC) is a powerful strategy that can elevate your brand's visibility and drive stronger results on Amazon alongside your Cost-Per-Acquisition (CPA) efforts. CPC campaigns offer sellers a unique opportunity to diversify their product offerings and open avenues to new partnerships.

Sellers should consider launching CPC campaigns to:

  1. **Unlock Incremental Inventory:** Unlock access to new creators as well as new channels and promotional strategies from existing partners.

  2. **Stand Out from the Competition** : Increase coverage with a unique offer for creators allowing them to earn on the qualified traffic they send to you.

  3. **Boost Your Organic Rank:** More coverage translates to more traffic. As your product pages see increased clicks and conversions, your organic rank on Amazon can improve, making your products more discoverable to potential customers.

  4. **Drive Long-Term Value (LTV):** CPC campaigns help generate awareness and interest in your brand, leading to sustained engagement over time. This can result in repeat customers and stronger brand loyalty.

  5. **Flexible Budgeting:** With CPC, you have more control over your spending. You can set budgets and adjust bids according to performance, ensuring you maximize your return on investment.

**Demo Walkthrough**

**Best Practices for Initial Set Up
**

  * When deciding to launch a CPC campaign we recommend starting with new product categories to increase awareness and excitement for that brand

  * CPC rates would ideally be above average EPC’s as this will, in most cases, unlock incremental inventory for creators.

    * EPC = Total commissions earned / Number of clicks

  * To start, we recommend setting fairly large budgets in-line with or above current average monthly spend to allow for inevitable incrementality ($5,000 - $10,000).

**FAQ**

  * **What happens to traffic once a CPC campaign ends or runs out of budget?**

  * Due to how Levanta communicates with Amazon’s API, CPC campaigns will remain live through the remainder of the day after the budget is reached. For this reason, we recommend setting budgets with ‘buffer’ room in mind.

  * Links will continue to track through Levanta once a CPC campaign expires due to budget or time constraints, and will revert to the CPA rate assigned to that ASIN

  * **Budget notifications?**

  * You will receive notifications when a campaign reaches 50, 75, and 100% of its allocated budget

  * **What Time Zone is my CPC campaign running in?**

  * Your CPC budget isn't timezone specific but the start and end date are based on the Amazon region of the product

  * **Can I edit my campaign?**

  * You can not currently edit your campaign so make sure all details are accurate before setting it live. The ability to edit campaigns will be coming soon!

## **What are Paid Placements?**

**Paid Placements** are sponsored content agreements where a brand pays a creator a fixed amount in exchange for specified, branded content. This can include social media posts, videos, newsletter features, blog posts, or other promotional methods agreed upon in advance.

Paid placements typically define:

  * The content type (e.g., Instagram Reel, TikTok video, newsletter feature)

  * The price

  * Any usage rights (permission to reuse the content)

  * Timelines for creation, content review, and posting

Levanta manages these agreements end-to-end so both parties have clarity and protection.

## **What Is a Rate Card?**

A **Rate Card** is a creator’s pricing menu for paid content. It shows:

  * The types of placements a creator offers

  * Their price for each placement

  * Separate pricing for usage rights, if applicable

As a seller, you can browse creator rate cards to understand quickly:

  * What content formats do they offer

  * Their requested prices

  * Availability and pricing for content usage rights

Creators only list placements they actually offer, so anything you see on a rate card is available for a proposal.

## _Adjusting Commissions for Deals_

Amazon's reported sales amount doesn't always reflect the discounted price a customer actually paid. For certain deal types, Amazon may still report the product's original price to Levanta even when the customer used a discount.

If Adjust Commissions isn't enabled, creator commissions may be calculated based on that original price. Enabling Adjust Commissions has Levanta automatically schedule a commission rate adjustment for the deal period based on the full price and discount you enter, so the final creator commission matches what the customer actually paid.

### When to Enable Adjust Commissions

Turn this on for:

  * Lightning Deals

  * Coupons

  * Promo Codes

These deal types' discounted prices aren't always reflected in Amazon's attribution reports, so enabling Adjust Commissions keeps creator commissions from being calculated on the full price.

You typically don't need it for:

  * Discounted Deals

  * Prime Day Deals

  * Limited-Time Deals

Amazon usually reports the correct discounted price for these, so commissions are already calculated based on what the customer paid.

💡 Note: Subscribe & Save doesn't currently support Deals or Adjust Commissions.

### How to Turn It On

When uploading a Deals CSV or creating a deal manually:

  * Set Adjust Public Commissions to Yes, or

  * Set Adjust Private Commissions to Yes

Levanta will automatically schedule the applicable commission rate adjustment for the deal period based on the discount you provide.

_Above: Clicking “adjust commissioning for these types of deals” in the deal form reveals the Adjust Public/Private Commissions checkboxes._

### What Happens When You Enable It

Levanta schedules a new, lower commission rate for the duration of the deal. Even though Amazon may still report the original price, the adjusted rate ensures the final creator commission matches the discounted price.

Example:

  * Full price: $100

  * Discounted price: $50

  * Original commission rate: 10%

Without Adjust Commissions:

$100 × 10% = $10 creator commission

With Adjust Commissions enabled, Levanta lowers the rate to 5% for the deal period:

$100 × 5% = $5 creator commission

That matches what you'd get calculating from the discounted price directly:

$50 × 10% = $5 creator commission

So you may see the commission rate drop from 10% to 5% during the deal. This isn't a cut to what creators earn — it's how Levanta keeps the final commission amount aligned with the discounted price, even when Amazon reports the full price.

_Above: A scheduled commission change, shown as it appears in the product notification bar._

### After the Deal Ends

Commissions revert to your previously scheduled settings. You can view scheduled commission adjustments on the [_product page_](https://app.levanta.io/seller/products/scheduled-commissions/public).

_Above: The Scheduled Commission Changes page, showing current commission, the scheduled change, and the associated deal._

### _Important Note:_

_Adjust Commissions only changes the creator commission rate and final commission amount during the deal period. It doesn't change the sale price reported by Amazon Attribution, so Sales or GMV in your Levanta dashboard may still reflect the full price. The platform's variable rate fee also continues to be calculated based on the GMV Amazon reports._

# Understanding Amazon’s Brand Referral Bonus (BRB) Program

Last updated 16 days ago

**Overview**

[ _Amazon’s Brand Referral Bonus_](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/hub/reference/external/GL9HPJ34VBFP76HX?initialSessionID=137-7590772-6016720&ld=NSGoogle) (BRB) program allows sellers to earn credits on qualified off-Amazon traffic. When you drive traffic to Amazon using an Attribution tag—such as those generated by Levanta—and that traffic results in a sale, Amazon may apply a bonus to your account.

These bonuses are credited toward future referral fees, helping you maximize the efficiency of your marketing spend.

However, it’s important to follow [all program requirements](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies/#Associates%20Program%20Participation%20Requirements) to remain eligible. Non-compliance may lead to forfeited bonuses or removal from the program.

**_How the BRB Bonus Works_**

Bonuses are calculated on a monthly basis and are applied as Amazon referral fee refunds. You can view these in the Payment Repository Report within your Amazon dashboard.

📌 Please note that bonuses take up to two months to process, as this accounts for potential returns or cancellations.

**How to View Your Brand Referral Bonus (BRB) Report in Levanta**

You can easily check your estimated Brand Referral Bonus earnings through Levanta’s reporting dashboard. Here’s how:

  * Navigate to the **Reports** tab in your Levanta account

  * Click on **More Reports** to open the Brand Referral Bonus report

  * You can also select your preferred date range to view bonus predictions over time

 _Note: These earnings are estimates based on tracked sales through your external marketing links. Actual payouts are handled through your Amazon Seller Central account._

#### **What Counts as Qualified Traffic and Purchases?**

**Qualified Traffic**
This refers to any off-Amazon traffic (like from social media, blogs, or other platforms) that’s directed to your product or brand store page using an Amazon Attribution tag generated through Levanta. As long as the click comes through one of these tagged links, it counts!

**Qualified Purchases**
These are purchases made by customers within 14 days after clicking on a Qualified Traffic link. As long as the purchase happens within that window, it contributes to your Brand Referral Bonus.

📌 _Tip: Always make sure you’re using the correct Levanta-generated link to ensure your traffic and purchases are properly tracked by Amazon._

### **Staying Compliant with Amazon's Brand Referral Bonus (BRB) & Amazon Associates Programs**

To make the most of Amazon’s Brand Referral Bonus (BRB) program while staying compliant, it’s essential to understand the key restrictions around earnings and traffic sources. Here's a breakdown:

**1\. Simultaneous Earnings – One Program Per Tag**

You can only earn either a Brand Referral Bonus or an Amazon Associates commission for a single tag created in[ _Amazon Attribution_](https://advertising.amazon.com/solutions/products/amazon-attribution).

If Amazon detects you're trying to claim both:

  * The bonus may be denied

  * You may be removed from one or both programs

  * In serious or repeated cases, your Amazon Seller or Associates account could be terminated

### **2\. Traffic Source Requirements**

Only traffic from approved, brand-safe sources will qualify for the bonus.

Amazon may reject traffic that violates its standards. For reference, see[ _Sections 1(a)–1(f) of the Amazon Associates Program Participation Requirements_](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/policies).

### **3\. Stay Compliant**

To earn and retain bonuses:

  * Use one program per tag

  * Ensure your traffic sources follow Amazon's guidelines

  * Review the[ _Brand Referral Bonus terms in Seller Central_](https://sellercentral.amazon.com/help/help.html?itemID=GULU6RSPWT6GZMMW)

# Sampling Suites: Let Creators Choose Their Own Sample

Last updated 1 day ago

* * *

### **Overview**

A **Sampling Suite** is a curated list of your products that you send to a creator, letting them pick the item they want instead of you picking for them. You build the suite once, then send it to as many creators as you like.

This cuts the back-and-forth of one-off sample requests: no more reviewing individual asks, guessing which size or color a creator wants, or leaving requests sitting in your queue.

Sampling Suites work for both **Amazon** and **Shopify** brands.

**Note:** _Product sampling — including Sampling Suites — is available only for_ **_Gold_** _and_ **_Enterprise_** _sellers. If you'd like to upgrade, please contact us at_ [**__cs@levanta.io__**](mailto:cs@levanta.io) _for further assistance._

### **Before You Start**

Sampling Suites use the same fulfillment infrastructure as standard product samples, so the same prerequisites apply.

**A Sampling Suite is tied to one brand,** and each brand belongs to a single marketplace. So a suite is either an Amazon suite or a Shopify suite — you can't mix products from both into one list. If you sell the same catalog on Amazon and Shopify, create a separate suite for each and send whichever fits the partnership.

**For Amazon brands:**

  * Your **Amazon Selling Partner connection** must be added under **Settings → Connections → Add Amazon Connection → Add Amazon Selling Partner Connection**.

⚠ _Be sure to choose this final option — the others are for Amazon Ads and will not connect your Selling Partner account._

  * The brand must have FBA inventory available for the products you want to include.

**For Shopify brands:**

  * Your **Shopify connection** must be added under **Settings → Connections → Shopify → Add Shopify Connection** , which installs the Levanta app from the Shopify App Store.

  * The brand must be activated in Levanta. See [_Managing Brands and Products in Levanta_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1167493894-activating-your-brand-and-setting-affiliate-terms).

For either marketplace, the brand must be **sample-eligible** before it will appear as an option when you create a suite.

### **Creating a Sampling Suite**

  1. Go to **Products → Samples** and select the **Sampling Suites** tab.

  2. Click **Create Sampling Suite**.

  3. Give the suite a name — something you'll recognize later, like "Spring Launch — Ella Bella" or "Top Creator Welcome Kit."

  4. Select the **brand** the suite belongs to. The brand you pick determines the marketplace — Amazon or Shopify — and only that brand's products will be available to add.

  5. Add products. Products are selected at the **variant level** , not the parent listing level — SKUs for Amazon brands, product variants for Shopify brands. That means if you want a creator to be able to choose a specific size or color, add each of those variants as its own line item.

  6. For **Amazon** suites, choose a **fulfillment preference** : **Cheapest** or **Fastest**. This determines the shipping speed used across every product in the suite. Shopify suites fulfill through your store's normal shipping setup, so there's no speed option to set.

  7. Save the suite.

**A few things to know while building:**

  * Levanta runs an eligibility check on each product as you add it. If a product can't be fulfilled, you'll see an error on that line — remove it before saving.

  * A suite must contain **at least one product**. You can't create or save a suite with an empty product list.

  * Suites are capped at **20 products**. This keeps eligibility checks fast so creators aren't waiting on a slow-loading page.

### **Sending a Sampling Suite to a Creator**

Once a suite is built, you can send it from a few places in Levanta:

  * The **Sampling Suites** tab, using the **Send** action on the suite

  * A creator's profile in **Creator Discovery**

  * Anywhere the existing **Send Sample** action appears

When you send, the creator receives the suite as a message in your Levanta message thread — the same place standard sample requests land.

### **What the Creator Sees**

The creator gets a Sampling Suite invite in their **Messages** and in their **Samples** tab. From there they can:

  * View every product in the suite

  * Confirm their shipping address

  * **Select one product** and accept

  * **Decline** the whole invite

When the creator selects a product, Levanta runs a final eligibility check on that item. If it passes, a standard product sample order is created and fulfilled automatically — through Amazon for Amazon suites, through your store for Shopify suites — exactly as it would for any other sample. If a specific product isn't available to them, it's disabled in the list and they're prompted to choose a different one.

### **Tracking and Managing Suites**

Sampling Suite invites appear alongside your other sample activity in the **Samples** tab, so you can see at a glance which invites are pending, accepted, or declined.

**Editing a suite.** You can update a suite's name, products, and fulfillment preference at any time from the **Sampling Suites** tab. Edits apply going forward; invites already accepted aren't affected.

**Cancelling an invite.** If a creator hasn't accepted yet, you can cancel the invite.

**Archiving a suite.** Archiving retires a suite so it can no longer be sent or redeemed. When you archive, Levanta will confirm which open invites will be cancelled as a result.

**Expiration.** Sampling Suite invites expire on the same schedule as standard product sample requests.

### **How Sampling Suite Orders Are Fulfilled & Reported**

Nothing changes here versus standard samples — fulfillment follows whichever marketplace the suite's brand belongs to.

**Amazon suites.** When a creator accepts a product, the order is processed through Amazon as a **Multi-Channel Fulfillment (MCF)** order:

  * Brands are billed **per sample unit sent**

  * All **standard FBA MCF fees** apply

  * Fulfillment is handled entirely by **Amazon** , using your existing FBA inventory

Because these are MCF orders, they **do not appear in standard Amazon Business Reports**. They're reflected in **MCF order reports** , which you pull separately from Amazon. Use Levanta to see which creators and products were sampled, then cross-reference with Amazon's MCF reports for a complete record.

**Shopify suites.** When a creator accepts a product, the sample is created as an order in your Shopify store and fulfilled through your existing shipping setup. The order appears in your Shopify admin alongside your other orders, so tracking lives there rather than in a separate report.

In both cases, Levanta's **Samples** tab remains your view of which creators received which products.

### **Sampling Suites vs. Standard Samples**

| **Standard sample**| **Sampling Suite**
---|---|---
Who picks the product| Seller (or creator requests a specific item)| Creator, from your curated list
Setup| Per creator, per product| Build once, send many times
Best for| A specific product you want promoted| Letting creators self-select the right fit — sizes, colors, categories

Sampling Suites work best for apparel, footwear, and any catalog where fit or preference matters — and for high-volume creator recruitment where reviewing individual requests doesn't scale.

**_Example_** _: If a Creator drives a sale on February 12, the invoice for that commission will be generated between March 1–3._

**Reconciliation Adjustments (Returns & Late Attribution)**

At times, Amazon may issue attribution adjustments, which impact how conversions are recorded. These adjustments usually happen in two scenarios:

#### **Returns and Rescinded Conversions:**

If you’ve already paid an invoice that includes a **commission for a conversion that is later rescinded** —such as a **product return** —within **30 days** of the invoice date, Levanta will:

  * Apply a**reconciliation adjustment** to your **next invoice**

  * This adjustment will offset the overpaid commission from the returned or canceled order

This ensures you're not charged for conversions that didn’t result in a final sale.

### **Late Attribution:**

Amazon Attribution typically has a **24–48 hour delay** in reporting conversions.

To ensure monthly invoices reflect all valid conversions, **Levanta waits 48 hours after the end of each month** before generating commission invoices. This allows us to capture sales that occurred in the final days of the month.

However, there are rare instances where Amazon attributes a sale to a Creator after that 48-hour window. When this happens:

  * The conversion may not appear on the current month’s invoice

  * Levanta will include a **reconciliation adjustment** to your **next invoice for** the missed commission to correct the underpayment

These adjustments are usually minor but help ensure all eligible commissions are eventually accounted for.

## Understanding the __Halo Effect__

Beyond direct conversions, Levanta helps you capture the **broader business impact** of your affiliate program — what's known as the **Halo Effect**. These are the indirect, secondary benefits your brand gains from creator-driven traffic, even when a sale doesn't happen immediately through a tracked link.

Halo benefits typically emerge after consistent program usage and significantly increase the perceived value of your investment. They support ROI beyond what's visible on a standard report, reduce resistance to commission changes, and drive long-term retention and brand advocacy.

### _Brand Awareness_

While brand awareness can't be measured directly through attribution stats alone, it shows up in observable Amazon behaviors. When attribution is used correctly, it reveals how off-Amazon media builds demand that can later convert organically — even if the sale doesn't happen right away.

**Key signals to watch in your reports:**

**_Detail Page Views (DPV)_**__ — A strong indicator of brand exposure and interest. High DPV with a lower conversion rate still signals valuable awareness-driven traffic.

**_Indirect SEO Lift_** __ — Creator traffic contributes to branded keyword searches on Amazon and increases organic traffic to your brand storefront.

**_New-to-Brand (NTB) Customers_** — NTB purchases represent first-time buyers to your brand in the last 12 months. A high NTB percentage means your campaigns are successfully reaching new audiences.

### _Best Seller Rank (BSR)_

**Best Seller Rank (BSR)** is Amazon's internal ranking showing how well a product sells compared to others in the same category. The lower the number, the better the rank. BSR is driven primarily by:

  * **Recent sales velocity** (most important factor)

  * **Sales history** , which smooths out ranking spikes

  * **Category-relative performance** against competitor ASINs

A few important things to keep in mind:

  * **Conversion quality matters more than traffic volume.** High-converting external traffic improves BSR; low-converting traffic can stall or decline it. Poor traffic quality dilutes overall performance.

  * **Timing is critical.** BSR is time-weighted — short sales spikes create temporary rank lifts, while sustained sales build a more stable BSR.

  * **Attribution sales count the same as organic or PPC.** There is no de-weighting or special labeling. A sale is a sale.

  * **Category competition sets the ceiling.** BSR is relative, so competitor sales velocity directly impacts how much rank movement is possible. Maintaining consistent sales is key to keeping pace.

### _Lifetime Value (LTV)_

Amazon Attribution helps identify **new-to-brand (NTB) vs. returning purchases** , making it a powerful tool for understanding customer lifetime value. Channels that drive higher NTB rates and repeat purchase behavior — like Levanta — are strong indicators of long-term LTV.

**Key LTV metrics to track in Levanta:**

  * New-to-Brand Purchases and Sales

  * NTB Order Rate

  * NTB Units per Order

**How to measure LTV signals on Amazon:**

  * Repeat Purchase Behavior

  * Reorder Frequency

  * Subscribe & Save Retention

  * Cross-ASIN Repurchase

>  _Channels like Levanta that drive higher NTB rates and repeat purchase behaviors deliver strong LTV returns over time._

### _Referring URL Report_

Shows performance by referring domain and URL — which external page sent the traffic that converted, down to the specific page rather than just the creator.

**_Displays:_**_Clicks, Conversions, and Sales by referring URL._

**Q: I’m not getting picked up by creators. How do I get creators to join my affiliate program?**

**A:** You’ll begin seeing traffic in Levanta after the first 10 clicks are recorded, and you’ll begin seeing conversions after the first sale. Keep in mind that Amazon Attribution data has a 24-hour delay, and thus it may take up to 24 hours for conversions/traffic to be displayed in Levanta.

**Q: When do I pay commission after a new conversion?**

**A:** Invoices are sent for conversions within the first few days of the month after a conversion was made. For example, if a Creator drove a commission on Feb 12th, you would be invoiced for that conversion between March 1st and 3rd. Levanta pays out commissions to your Creators within 30 days after the date you were invoiced.

**Q: What happens if I pay commission to a Creator for a product that is later returned?**

**A:** Levanta tracks returns and other scenarios where a conversion is rescinded. If you pay an invoice that includes a commission for a conversion that is later rescinded within 30 days of the date the invoice was sent, we will adjust your following invoice to account for the overpayment. After 30 days from when your invoice was sent, all conversions in that invoice are considered “locked” and adjustments will no longer be made.

**Q: How do I view conversion-level data?**

**A:** While you can review aggregate conversion data in our reporting suite, due to the level of detail offered by Amazon’s Attribution API, we cannot provide in-depth conversion-level data at this time.

**Q: Can I pay Creators outside of the platform?**

**A:** We do not support paying Creators outside of the platform for conversions that are tracked within Levanta.

**Q: How do I create custom reports?**

**A:** We do not currently offer custom reports. Please contact customer success via in-app chat to request new reports you would like to see supported in the app.

# Understanding Your Notifications (Sellers)

Last updated 16 days ago

Stay informed with timely updates that help you manage your campaigns, partnerships, and performance within Levanta. Below is a breakdown of the key notifications sellers may receive and what each one means.

## 🔎 Filtering Your Notifications

Your Notifications panel supports filtering, so you can quickly narrow down what you're looking at instead of scrolling through everything. Click the filter icon at the top of the panel to filter by:

  * Urgency – priority level of the notification

  * Category – notification type, such as Partnerships, Payments, Digest, Commissions, or Teams

  * Marketplace – a specific connected marketplace

Use Clear All to dismiss every notification at once, or the ⚙ settings icon to manage your notification preferences.

## 🤝 Partnerships

###  __Partnership Request Received__

You've received a new creator application for your brand's program.

**_What to do:_**_Review and approve/decline via the Partnerships tab in your dashboard._

### __Invite Accepted__

A creator has accepted your partnership invitation.

**_What to do:_**_Monitor their performance and consider sending sample products or CPC invites to activate the partnership._

##  💳 Payments

###  __Paid Placement Refund Processed__

Sent when a refund for your Paid Placement contract has been processed.

**_What to know:_**_Allow 5–10 business days for the refund to be credited back to your card._

### __Paid Placement Escrow Payment Failed__

Sent when your Paid Placement escrow payment fails to process.

**What to do:** Retry your payment as soon as possible to avoid disruption to your Paid Placement campaign.

### __Set up Payment Method Reminder__

Sent weekly if no payment method has been configured.

**_What to do:_**_Add your preferred payment method in your Billing Settings to avoid delays in creator payouts or platform access._

##  💰 Commissions

### ** __CPC Budget Threshold Alerts__**

Get notified when your Cost-Per-Click campaign hits these milestones:

  * 50%

  * 75%

  * 100% of the total campaign budget

** _What to do:_**_Review performance and consider adjusting the budget or strategy if needed._

### __Creator Connection Campaign Expiring Soon__

Sent as one of your active Creator Connections campaigns approaches its end date (e.g., "expires in 2 days").

**_What to do:_**_Review the campaign and extend or renew it if you'd like it to keep running without a gap in coverage._

##  📬 Digest

### ** __Daily Summary__**

A daily email report summarizing important platform activity, including:

  * New creator applications

  * Invite acceptances

  * Campaign activity

  * Budget status updates

Use this to stay on top of your program's performance and creator engagement.

### **__Products Archived__**

One or more of your active products can no longer be found via your Amazon connection and have been **automatically archived** (previously, this only surfaced as a "not found" flag rather than an automatic archive).

**_What to do:_**_Check your catalog on Amazon to confirm product availability, then unarchive the product in Levanta if it's active again. These are reviewed daily._

##  👥 Teams

Notifications under the Teams category relate to account management and collaboration between team members. While specific notification text can vary, common alerts in this category include:

  * New Team Member Invitations – Sent when an Admin invites a new person to join the brand's Levanta account.

  * Invitation Acceptances – Confirms a newly invited team member has successfully joined and activated their account.

  * Role & Permission Changes – Sent when a user's role (e.g., changing from "Member" to "Admin") has been modified by an account owner.

  * Security & Login Alerts – High-urgency notifications for unusual login attempts, or when account security settings (like Stripe setup for creators) need attention.

  * Platform Access Requirements – Flags for missing access permissions, such as needing specific credentials for the Levanta Admin Panel or Seller Central integrations.

## **_Other Sample Notifications_**

###  _Amazon Ads Connection Disconnected_

Sent immediately when your Amazon Ads connection is lost.

**_What to know:_**_You have a 7-day grace period to reconnect before campaigns are paused and CPC functionality is disabled._

### _Brand Connection Disconnected_

Sent when a brand's connection to Amazon is lost (e.g., attribution).

**_What to know:_**_A 7-day buffer is in place before creator access is disabled and brand visibility is paused._

### _CC (Creator Connections) Onboarding Reminder_

Sent weekly if Creator Connections is enabled, but no brand is connected to the feature.

**_What to do:_**_Complete onboarding by linking a brand to begin inviting creators and receiving applications._

### _Plan Cancellation Notice_

Sent multiple times leading up to a scheduled plan cancellation.

**What to know:** These alerts allow time for you to update billing details or switch plans before deactivation occurs.

## _📌 Where You'll Receive Notifications?_

All notifications are sent via email and appear in your Levanta dashboard. Staying on top of these alerts ensures your creator program runs smoothly and efficiently.

# Amazon Attribution Integration

Last updated 6 months ago

### **Amazon Attribution Integration: Troubleshooting & FAQ**

If you're experiencing issues connecting your Amazon Seller Central account or accessing attribution data in Levanta, this guide walks you through common troubleshooting steps and how to resolve them.

**Why Can’t I Connect My Seller Central Account?**

Make sure of the following:

✅ You're using the correct email address associated with the Seller Central account you intend to connect.

✅ You are the primary (top-level) user on the Seller Central account.

✅ If you are a secondary user, confirm you have been granted the correct permissions (see instructions below).

✅ Your brand is Brand Registered and has access to Amazon Attribution.

**Why Does Levanta Show “Brand Does Not Have Attribution Access”?**

If you're seeing the error message _"Brand does not have Amazon Attribution access"_ in Levanta, it means your Amazon Seller Central account is either not fully set up with the required permissions, or there's an issue with your brand’s enrollment in Amazon Attribution.

How to Fix the “Attribution Access” Error:

**Contact Amazon Seller Support**

  * Ask to enable **Amazon Attribution API access** for your brand.

  * Mention that you are trying to integrate with a **third-party partner (Levanta)** that relies on Attribution data.

  * For additional support, please contact [ads-api-support@amazon.com](mailto:ads-api-support@amazon.com)

**Verify Brand Registry Enrollment**

  * Confirm that your brand is enrolled in Brand Registry and that your Seller Central account is correctly associated with the brand.

**Allow for Sync Time**

  * If you've recently updated settings, gained permissions, or enrolled in Attribution, please allow **24–48 hours** for the data to sync with Levanta.

📝**_Note:_**_Levanta cannot modify your Amazon settings directly. All permission updates must be handled through your Amazon Seller Central account or with assistance from Amazon Seller Support._

# Managing Brands & Products in Levanta

Last updated 16 days ago

## **How to Activate, Disable, Archive, and Manage Your Brands and Products**

This guide walks you through the key actions to manage your brands, products, and storefronts in the Levanta platform.

### **Activating Your Brand**

After filling out your [**_Brand Detail Page_**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/7948411124-general-account-setup), follow these steps to make your brand visible to creators:

#### **Steps:**

  1. Navigate to **Brands** from the left-hand menu.

  2. Click on the brand you want to activate.

  3. Click the **"Activate"** button to launch the activation widget.

  4. **Review Affiliate Program Terms** :

     * You can use Levanta’s default affiliate terms or choose “Use custom terms” to link to your own hosted terms.

  5. Acknowledge the geo-activation notice.

  6. (Optional) Click **Advanced Options** to turn on **Auto-Accept Creator Applications**.

## **Where New Products Appear in Levanta**

When new products are added to your store, they may not always appear immediately in your active product list. Depending on your setup, new products will either:

  * **A)** Appear under **Archived Products** , where you'll need to **manually unarchive** them to make them visible and active, or

  * **B)** Require a **manual import** using the **Update Products via CSV** option.

**To Manually Import Products**

If your new products do not appear under **Archived Products** , you can manually import them:

  1. Navigate to **Products**

  2. Click **Update Products via CSV** at the top right of the page.

  3. Upload your updated CSV file to sync new products into your catalog.

  4. Once imported, they'll display under your **active product list**.

### **Deactivating a Brand**

**Deactivating a brand will:**

  * Cancel all active creator partnerships.

  * Disable all products under the brand.

  * Archive existing links.

  * Remove visibility from the Creator Marketplace.

**To Deactivate:**

  * Go to **Brands**.

  * Click on the brand you want to disable.

  * Scroll to the bottom of the brand detail page.

  * Click "_Deactivate Brand_ ".

⚠ _Use with caution — This action affects all linked entities._

### **Archiving a Brand**

 _Archiving_ hides inactive or unwanted brands (especially those synced from Amazon) from your visible list.

**To Archive:**

  1. Navigate to **Brands**.

  2. Select the brand(s) you'd like to archive.

  3. Click **Actions → Archive Brand**.

## **How to Archive and Unarchive a Product**

Levanta gives you the ability to archive products you no longer wish to actively display or manage—and just as easily, unarchive them when needed. Here’s how:

### **To Unarchive a Product**

  1. From the **Products** tab, scroll to the bottom of the product list.

  2. Click on **See Archived Products**.

  3. Locate the archived product(s) you'd like to restore.

  4. Select the checkbox and click **Unarchive** under the **Actions** menu.

### **To Archive a Product**

  1. Navigate to the **Products** tab in your left-hand menu.

  2. Locate the product you’d like to archive.

  3. Click the checkbox next to the product and choose **Actions** → **Archive Products**.

## **Activating Storefront Pages**

Navigate to **Brands** and select the brand you want to work with. In the **“Brand Profile”** section, click on the gear icon and choose “[** _Manage Storefronts URL_**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/7948411124-general-account-setup)” to edit your Storefront settings. You will see a list of your Storefronts pulled in from Amazon. From here you can do the following:.

  * Toggle ON/OFF to make a page available or unavailable to creators.

  * Toggle ON/OFF to make a particular page your default main Storefront link.
