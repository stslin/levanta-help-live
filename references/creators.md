# Getting Started with Levanta: A Comprehensive Guide for Creators

Last updated 4 days ago

## **Welcome to Levanta!**

Levanta empowers creators to connect with brands, promote products, and maximize your earnings. This guide will walk you through the essential steps to get started as a Levanta Creator, from applying to the platform to managing your account and team.

## **Apply to Join the Levanta Platform**

If you haven’t already joined Levanta, visit[ Levanta’s application portal](https://app.levanta.io/) to get started.

  1. Create an account to access the platform application form.

  2. Fill out your basic details and click **“Submit Application.”**

  3. Once your application is submitted, the Levanta team will review your profile.

Every creator application undergoes a thorough review by our team to ensure quality and alignment with our platform. You can review our account guidelines [here](https://knowledge.levanta.io/why-wasnt-my-creator-account-accepted). While waiting for approval, you can explore the platform and begin setting up your account.

## Setting Up Your Public Creator Profile

Having a complete Creator profile is essential for attracting brands. Profiles with a photo, bio, website, and email address are more likely to secure partnerships.

To set up your Creator profile:

  1. Go to **“Settings”** and click on the **“Team”** tab.

  2. Update the following details:

     * **Profile Image** : Hover over your profile picture and click to upload a new image so that brands can see the face behind the creator!

     * **Public Details** : Click **“Update Team Profile”** to edit your Company Name, Bio, Promotion Regions, Website, and Email Address.

  3. Make sure your **Creator Profile** clearly outlines what you do so brands can easily learn more about your content and promotional efforts. You can edit this by going to "**Settings** " and clicking the "**Team** " tab. Then, click "**Update Creator Profile**."

## **Adding Team Members and Managing Permissions**

You must add any additional team members to your profile directly from your existing dashboard. If they navigate through the sign-up flow, an entirely new account will be created with no access to your existing account and data.

### **Invite New Team Members**

  1. Navigate to **“Settings”** and click the **“Team”** tab.

  2. Scroll down to the **“Team Invites”** section.

  3. Select **“Invite New Member,”** enter their email address, and click **“Invite.”**

The invited member will receive an email with a link to set up their Levanta account. Learn more by reading our guide on [**Understanding Team Members, User Roles & Permissions.**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1294965172-understanding-user-roles-permissions?fbclid=IwY2xjawQlQHBleHRuA2FlbQIxMABicmlkETFmcWVhNlNpSDFQMVMyYkVhc3J0YwZhcHBfaWQQMjIyMDM5MTc4ODIwMDg5MgABHr6f8XbAEutokvfttQtQ3X66IRCrW7W1UN5rd34KDYUtwMNd90tJ4nOFn_fy_aem__hDHDDvrU-aoPIrVTb1HMA)

### **User Types and Permissions**

Levanta offers two user types:

  * **Admin** : Full access, including Payments, adding team members, and assigning permissions.

  * **Member** : Limited access; cannot manage Payments, team members, or permissions.

By default, new members are assigned the **“Member”** role.

To change a team member’s role:

  1. Go to **“Settings”** and navigate to the **“Team Members”** section.

  2. Click the edit icon next to the member’s name.

  3. Select the new role from the dropdown menu and click **“Save.”**

### **Customizing Notifications**

It is important to stay updated about activity across your account and partnerships. You have the option to customize your notifications based on your preferences on the platform.

  1. Go to **“Settings”** then **“Notifications.”**

  2. Toggle to "off" if you don't want to receive a Daily Activity Summary.

## **Setting Up Payments**

### **Getting Paid – Setting Up Stripe**

Levanta partners with **Stripe** to make payments seamless and automatic. To set up your payment account:

  1. Navigate to the **“Payments”** tab in the menu and click **“Set Up Payments.”**

  2. You will be redirected to the Levanta-linked Stripe portal. Follow these steps:

     * Create a Stripe account. Levanta uses a bespoke version of Stripe so you’ll need to set up your account even if you work with Stripe elsewhere.

     * Provide a valid phone number.

     * Enter your bank account or debit card information.

     * Submit your tax/identification number (such as EIN or SSN).

  3. After completing the setup, you’ll be redirected to Levanta.

(At this time, Stripe is the only accepted payment method. Please make sure you are in a region that accepts Stripe payments.)

You’re now ready to start receiving monthly payments from your promotional efforts!

## **Once Approved, What’s Next?**

Levanta brands sell across three marketplaces: Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify (direct-to-consumer). You can browse and promote brands on any of them from the same dashboard.

After your account is approved, you’ll unlock a range of tools and opportunities designed to help you thrive as a Levanta Creator.

  1. **Explore Brand Partnerships and Promotions** \- To learn more about finding products and brands to promote**,**[**click here**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/find-products-to-promote)**.**

  2. Start **Creating and Implementing Links** in Content - To learn more about creating and managing links, [**click here**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/generating-and-managing-affiliate-links-in-levanta).

  3. Utilize **Platform Tools** \- To learn more about reporting, [**click here**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/reporting-1).

## **Get Familiar with Our Guidelines**

Maintaining professionalism and ethical standards is key to success on Levanta. All creators are expected to adhere to these core guidelines:

  1. **Promote Products Ethically and Accurately**

     * Ensure all promotional content is honest and transparent. Misleading claims or exaggerated promises are strictly prohibited.

     * Respect intellectual property rights and always represent brands truthfully.

     * No product purchase is necessary. If you want to receive products, you can either request a sample from the product page or communicate with the brands directly via the Levanta messaging console and ask for a sample. It's still the brand's choice whether they can/will send the product because it is not an expectation/requirement for brands to do this.

  2. **Comply with Platform Rules and Policies**

     * Follow all terms of service, community guidelines, and policies outlined by Levanta.

     * Stay updated on any changes to policies to ensure ongoing compliance.

  3. **Deliver High-Quality Content**

     * Create engaging, polished content that aligns with your audience’s interests and the brand’s values.

     * Tailor your approach for each campaign to maintain authenticity and credibility.

By following these guidelines, you’ll not only build successful partnerships but also establish trust and long-term relationships with brands and your audience.

### **Need Additional Help?**

If you have questions or need further assistance, our team is here to help!

# Why wasn’t my creator account accepted?

Last updated 4 days ago

_Last updated August 11, 2026_

Levanta has a rigorous approval process to ensure we continue to build a network of high**-** quality partnerships.

We are currently only accepting creators in the US, Canada, France, Germany, Italy, Mexico, Spain, the Netherlands, and the UK, where we have seller coverage on our platform.

For influencers and content creators, we are currently only approving those with published content and an organic, active, and engaged audience base with at least 10k followers on a single platform.

We do not approve creators whose content or accounts involve:

  * Obscene or pornographic content

  * Content that promotes illegal, violent, or hateful activities

  * Content that is abusive or discriminatory

  * Content that targets children under the age of 13

  * Content or social media accounts that duplicate other sites or people, or use a domain that contains other companies’ names or brands, or misspellings thereof

  * Content or sites that generate clicks through incentivized, automated, or artificial traffic

  * AI-generated personas and virtual influencers, or accounts where the content is primarily AI-generated. The creator featured in the content must be a real, identifiable person

  * Promotion of illicit substances, including CBD and THC. Note: Promotion of such substances may also result in withheld payments

Approvals are at the discretion of the team, and our policies and guidelines may change at any time. These guidelines apply to existing creators as well as new applicants. Accounts found to be in violation may be removed from the platform.

If your account wasn’t accepted, you’re encouraged to:

  * **Review and Resubmit** : If your application was declined for missing or incomplete information, double-check it against our requirements and submit again. Applications declined for the content guidelines above are not eligible for resubmission.

  * **Contact Our Support Team** : If you have questions about your application or believe it was declined in error, we’re here to help.

# Amazon Associates Policy Update FAQ

Last updated 4 days ago

This FAQ will help answer questions around the December 20, 2024, Amazon Associates Policy update.

## What is happening?

Effective **December 20, 2024,** Amazon is updating its [**Associates Program Operating Agreement**](https://affiliate-program.amazon.com/help/operating/compare)**** to prohibit creators from earning commissions on the same traffic through multiple programs, including adding Associates tags to Levanta links.

## How does this impact me?

Levanta links will no longer support Amazon Associates tags. This means that Creators will need to choose whether to use links from Levanta OR Amazon Associates on a product-by-product basis. When a product is available on Levanta, we recommend using _only_ Levanta links to maximize your earnings, given Levanta’s significantly higher commission rates. For products not yet available on Levanta, you should continue using your Associates tag as usual.

Please carefully review the information below, based on how you generate Levanta links:

  * If you’re using our mobile deeplinks (links that start with lvnta.com), no action is required; we’ll automatically remove any Associates tags after December 20th.

  * If you’re using our expanded links (links that look like [amazon.com/us/ASIN/parameters](http://amazon.com/us/ASIN/parameters) but are pulled from the dashboard), you will need to regenerate and repost your links by December 20th.

  * If you’re using our API, links generated via the API will no longer append Associates tags after December 20th. If you were previously appending Associates tags via API, please generate and post new links before December 20th.

  * If you’re appending Associates tags outside of the Levanta dashboard or API, we strongly recommend discontinuing this practice by December 20th.

After **December 20th** , any attempts to include your Associates tag in a Levanta link could result in conflicts with Amazon’s updated policies.

If you do not currently append Associates tags to your Levanta links, you can disregard this information as no further action is required.

## Why is Amazon making this change?

Amazon has updated its policies to ensure clarity and prevent duplication in commission structures across programs. This change is designed to simplify how traffic is attributed, focusing on a single monetization source per link.

## What if I need help making these changes to my links?

The Levanta team is here to help with these changes. If you have any additional questions, please reach out to your account manager or cs@levanta.io.

If you would like a more automated solution, we recommend **Geniuslink** , a trusted tool for managing and optimizing affiliate links.

As part of this transition, GeniusLink is offering a **60-day free trial** exclusively for Levanta affiliates who want assistance with:

  * **Removing Associates tags** from existing Levanta links.

  * **Prioritizing attribution tags** through Levanta over Associates tags when both are present.

This offer allows you to streamline your affiliate setup and ensure compliance with Amazon’s new policies, all while optimizing your link management.

If you’re interested in this offer, please reach out to help@geniuslink.com to sign up for the free trial and start making the necessary updates today.

## How Does Levanta Continue to Provide Value and Growth for Creators?

Levanta remains a distinct advantage for creators. Here’s why:

  * **Incremental Revenue Opportunities** : Creators can earn through both **CPA (cost per acquisition)** and **CPC (cost per click)** campaigns, maximizing revenue potential beyond just conversions.

# Generating and Managing Affiliate Links in Levanta

Last updated 4 days ago

Create, track, and optimize your Levanta affiliate links across Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify. Links work slightly differently on each marketplace, so find yours below.

## How Levanta links work

Levanta links are generated per product, so each product, including each variant, needs its own link. Individual product links tend to yield the highest conversions and best performance.

You can add tracking IDs to any link to see how it performs by article, channel, or campaign. These are visible only to you.

To find links you've already created, go to **Links** in your dashboard. Use **Copy** to retrieve one, or **Edit** to change it.

Once your link is created, publish it like any affiliate link: social media, blogs, emails, newsletters, wherever you post.

## Amazon

  * **Product identifier:** ASIN

  * **Clicks before your data appears:** 10 Click Minimum

  * **Cookie window:** 14 days

  * Links are mobile-optimized by default, so they open in the Amazon app

  * Storefront links are available where the brand has enabled them

**Full guide:** [_Generating Amazon Affiliate Links_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/6018990442-generating-amazon-affiliate-links)

## Walmart

  * **Product identifier:** Item ID, the Walmart equivalent of an ASIN

  * **Clicks before your data appears:** No minimum

  * **Cookie window:** 30 days

  * You can stack other promotions, such as coupon codes, alongside Walmart links

**Full guide:** [_Levanta's Walmart Integration for Creators_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/8228007165-levanta-s-walmart-integration-for-creators#create-affiliate-links)

## Shopify / DTC

  * **Product identifier:** product URL

  * **Clicks before your data appears:** No minimum

  * **Attribution window:** 14 days

  * Each brand sets its own return and reconciliation window of 30, 60, or 90 days. This affects when your commission finalizes, not whether the sale attributes to you

**Full guide:** [_Levanta's Shopify Integration for Creators_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/1330293883-levanta-s-shopify-integration-for-creators#create-affiliate-links)

For further assistance, don't hesitate to contact  _cs@levanta.io_.

# Discovering Products & Brands to Promote

Last updated 2 months ago

To get started, navigate to the **Discovery** tab in the navigation bar. This is your central hub for finding everything on Levanta, including the **Product Catalog** , **Brand Catalogue** , **Deals** , **Featured Products** , and **Lists**.

# **Product Catalog**

The Product Catalog is where you can browse, search, and filter all products available across Levanta's network. Navigate to Discovery and select All Categories to access the full catalog and unlock filter options.

## **Search**

The Search function is a powerful tool for quickly finding specific products. You can search by:

  * **ASIN**

  * **Brand**

  * **Keyword**

  * **Product Category**

  * **Product Name**

  * **Global Marketplace**

## **Filters**

Filtering helps narrow down products based on your preferences. You can filter products by (To see filter functions, make sure you click on "All Categories" when in Discovery):

  * **Marketplace:** Filter by marketplace to find products available on Amazon US, Amazon UK, Amazon Canada, Amazon Germany, Amazon France, Walmart, or Shopify.

  * **Brand:** View products from a specific brand.

  * **Commission Min:** Find products that fit within your desired commission range. For example, enter 15% to find only products with 15% commission or higher.

  * **Availability:** Show only in-stock products (this is the default setting).

  * **Access:** Automatically shows all products, but you can choose to display only products from brands you have partnered with. To gain access, apply to the brand and await approval.

  * **Price:** Filter products by price range.

  * **Featured Products:** Show only products that have been featured by Levanta.

  * **Sample Eligible Products:** Show only products available for sample requests.

  * **Deals:** Filter by deal status - Any, All, Active, or Upcoming.

  * **Min Deal Percent:** Set a minimum discount percentage for deal products.

  * **Deal Date:** Filter products by a specific deal date.

_Product Filters panel_

 _Note: If you search an ASIN and a different variation populates (i.e., a different color, size, or count), this likely means we have the ASIN available on Levanta, but it's nested under a parent ASIN. To find this, click into the product that populates upon search, scroll down, and search for the correct variant from the list._

# **Brand Catalog**

Explore the active brands on Levanta by navigating to **Discovery** and selecting **Brand Catalogue**. Here, you can browse all available brands and explore by category.

## **New Brand Discovery Experience**

The Brand Catalogue features a refreshed visual layout designed to make it easier and faster to find brands that match your niche and content style.

Each brand appears as a branded tile card that combines lifestyle imagery with the brand's logo, giving you a clearer sense of each brand's identity and aesthetic before you even click in.

_Brand Catalogue - branded tile cards with lifestyle imagery and commission rates_

Each tile also displays the commission rate range in the top corner, so you can quickly evaluate earning potential at a glance.

This makes it easier to:

  * Identify brands in your niche faster and with more context

  * Understand a brand's aesthetic and product category before clicking in

  * Evaluate commission rates without needing to open each brand individually

## **Brand Details**

Click on a brand's name to access its Brand Detail Page, where you can:

  * **View Product Catalog:** Scroll to the bottom of the page to explore their active products.

  * **Apply to Promote:** Click "Apply for Access" in the top right to request access to generate links for their products.

  * **View Brand Details:** Learn about the brand's background and Amazon storefront.

  * **Marketplace:** Identify which Amazon geographic regions the brand operates in.

## **Sort Options**

Use the "Best-Sellers" drop-down to re-order displayed products quickly based on:

  * **Best Sellers**

  * **Newly Added**

  * **Commission**

  * **Price**

# Generating Amazon Affiliate Links

Last updated 4 days ago

How to create and manage Levanta links for Amazon products. For Walmart and Shopify, see Generating and Managing Affiliate Links in Levanta.

## The 10-click threshold

Each Amazon Levanta link has a 10-click threshold, meaning performance metrics will only be visible after the link receives at least 10 unique clicks. Any activity before reaching this threshold is still tracked but remains inaccessible until the threshold is met.

Clicks typically show up within 24 hours, and conversions can take up to 48 hours to fully process and display in Levanta.

Walmart and Shopify links have no click threshold.

## Link format

By default, Amazon Levanta links are mobile-optimized, ensuring that mobile users are seamlessly directed to the Amazon app for a better purchasing experience. These links typically follow this format:

<https://lvnta.com/lv_XXXXXX>

If you prefer a non-mobile-optimized version, you can untick the Mobile Optimized Link option during link creation. This will generate a standard Amazon link, retaining all Levanta tracking parameters. If you are using a third-party link-shortening or management service (e.g., [Bit.ly](http://Bit.ly), TinyURL, URLGenius), you should use the expanded, non-mobile-optimized version of the link to ensure proper tracking.

## Generating a link

  1. **Navigate to the Product Page:** In your Levanta dashboard, go to Discover > Product Catalog or Partnerships > My Products and select the product for which you'd like to generate a link. You can also search for an ASIN in either section.

  2. **Create a New Link:** Scroll to the Create New Link button below the Product Details section.

# Getting Started with Paid Placements (For Creators)

Last updated 4 months ago

Your rate card tells brands what you offer and what you charge. Once it's set up, brands can browse your rates and send you placement proposals directly through Levanta. No DMs, no back-and-forth over email.

This article walks you through how to set up your rate card, including standard placements, custom placements, and usage rights pricing.

## **What Is a Rate Card?**

A rate card is your pricing menu for paid content. It lists the types of placements you offer (like an Instagram Reel or a newsletter mention), what you charge for each, and your rates for usage rights if a brand wants to repurpose your content.

You don't need to fill out every field. Only add placements you actually offer. Brands will only see what you've priced out.

## **Setting Up Standard Placements**

Standard placements are the most common content types brands look for, organized by platform. You can set a fixed price for each. These prices are your starting point. Brands may propose different terms, and you can always negotiate.

Here's what you can price out for each platform:

### **Instagram**

  * Feed Post

  * Reel

  * Story

  * Carousel

### **TikTok**

  * Video

  * Story

### **YouTube**

  * Video with Link in Description

### **Facebook**

  * Post

### **Pinterest**

  * Pin

### **LinkedIn**

  * Feed Post

For each placement you offer, enter your price. Leave any placements you don't offer blank; they won't appear on your rate card.

## **Adding Custom Placements**

Custom placements let you offer content types that fall outside the standard options. These might include:

  * Newsletter inclusions

  * Blog posts or listicles

  * Podcast mentions

  * Product reviews

  * Any other placement unique to your content

To add a custom placement:

  1. Enter the placement name

  2. Enter your price

  3. Enter your usage rights price (if applicable)

You can add as many custom placements as you like, but we recommend keeping your list focused. A shorter, clearer menu makes it easier for brands to understand what you offer and move forward quickly.

## **Setting Usage Rights Pricing**

Usage rights permit brands to repurpose your content beyond the original post. This might include:

  * Reposting on their own social media channels

  * Boosting the content on their social media with paid ad spend

  * Running the content as a paid ad on other platforms

  * Featuring the content on their website

  * Including the content in email marketing

  * Using the content in print advertising

Usage rights are priced separately from the placement itself. When you set up your rate card, you'll enter a usage rights price for each placement you offer. This is your starting rate; the specific channels the brand wants to use, and for how long, will be negotiated when they send a proposal.

For example, you might charge more for usage rights on a video (like a TikTok or Reel) than for a static image post, since video content typically has higher production value.

## **What Happens After You Publish Your Rate Card**

Once your rate card is live, brands on Levanta can view your rates and send you placement proposals.

Here's how the process works:

**1\. Brand Sends a Proposal.** You'll receive a notification when a brand wants to work with you. Their proposal will include the placement they're interested in, their offered price, and any usage rights requests (including duration and intended channels).

**2\. You Review and Respond** You can accept the proposal as-is, counter with different terms, or decline. Negotiations happen directly in Levanta. No need to jump to email or DMs.

For example:

  * Your rate card lists an Instagram Reel at $10,000

  * A brand proposes $8,000 for the Reel, plus $1,000 for 60 days of usage rights

  * You counter with $9,000 for the Reel and $1,100 for 30 days of usage rights

  * The brand accepts, and you move forward

**3\. Contract Is Generated Automatically.** Once both sides agree, Levanta generates a contract based on the agreed terms. No paperwork, no chasing signatures.

**4\. You Create and Submit Content.** You'll see clear next steps on what's due and when. Submit your content for brand review within the required timeframe.

**5\. Brand Reviews** The brand can approve your content or request one round of revisions. If revisions are requested, you'll have a set number of days to make edits and resubmit.

**6\. Final Approval and Posting** Once the brand gives final approval, you post the content and submit the link through Levanta.

## **Response Deadlines**

To keep deals moving, Levanta has built in deadlines at key stages:

  * **Responding to a proposal:** You have five (5) business days to respond to an initial proposal from a brand. If you don't respond, the proposal will expire automatically.

  * **Submitting content:** Once a contract is agreed upon, you'll have a set number of days to submit your content for review. The default is five (5) business days, but this can be negotiated as part of the deal.

  * **Submitting revisions:** If the brand requests changes, you'll have a set number of days to resubmit. The default is two (2) days, but this can be negotiated (two days is the minimum).

  * **Brand review of revisions:** Once you submit revisions, the brand has five (5) days to review and confirm. If they don't respond within that window, your revisions are automatically accepted, and you'll be expected to post the content.

These deadlines help protect both you and the brand from deals that stall out. You'll receive reminders as deadlines approach.

## **Tips for Setting Up Your Rate Card**

**Price for your starting point, not your floor.** Your rate card prices are where negotiations begin. It's okay if brands propose lower. You can always counter.

**Only list what you actually offer.** A focused rate card is easier for brands to navigate. If you don't use YouTube, don't fill out that section.

**Think about usage rights strategically.** Video content typically commands higher usage fees than static posts. Consider what it would take for you to feel good about a brand running your content as an ad.

**Keep custom placements clear.** If you add a custom placement, make sure the name is self-explanatory. "Newsletter Feature" is clearer than "Email Promo."

**Posting Content.** Make sure to post the content you created. A placement is considered complete only after the content has been posted and the product has been promoted.

## **What Are Brand Discount Codes?**

Brand discount codes let you offer your audience real savings on products from brands you're already partnered with on Levanta. Unlike a standard affiliate link, a code gives your followers something tangible: a percentage off or a fixed dollar amount saved at checkout.

**Shopify / DTC brands only.** Custom codes built on Levanta are available for brands running a direct-to-consumer store on Shopify. They are not currently available for Amazon-only partnerships.

## **Why Use Discount Codes?**

**Strengthen your creator brand:** Having a code signals a real, direct relationship with the brand, not just a generic affiliate link. It makes you look like a trusted partner, not just a promoter.

**Earn in channels where links don't work:** Tracking links are great for blog posts and Instagram stories, but they don't work everywhere. Discount codes can fill that gap. Say it on a podcast, mention it in an in-feed video. You can also use a code alongside a tracking link to cover all your bases.

## **How It Works**

  * **A brand creates a code and assigns it to you.** Codes are set up by the brand and tied specifically to you. One creator per code.

  * **You're notified.** Your code appears in the Promo Codes tab (Tracking > Promo Codes in your sidebar). In-app notifications are coming soon.

  * **You promote it however it fits your content.** Drop it in a caption, say it on a podcast, pin it in your bio, or include it alongside your affiliate link in a story or post.

## **Finding and Managing Your Codes**

Go to Tracking > Promo Codes in your sidebar. Your codes table shows the brand name, the code itself, eligible products (click any thumbnail to see the full list), and the discount type. You can filter by brand, minimum discount percentage, or minimum discount amount, and sort by total uses or total sales.

## **How Commission Attribution Works**

Each brand chooses one of two attribution settings. This is a brand-wide setting, not configured per code or per creator. It only comes into play when two different creators are both connected to the same conversion.

If only one creator is involved in the sale, that creator gets paid, regardless of which setting the brand has chosen.

_Attribution Priority setting - brands choose this once_ ,_and it applies across all creators and codes_

**Tracking link wins (default):** Credit goes to the creator whose link the customer last clicked. If a customer clicks your link and buys, you earn commission whether or not they used a code at checkout. _Example: a customer clicks Creator A's link, then enters Creator B's coupon code. Under this setting, Creator A gets the commission._

**Discount code wins:** Credit goes to the creator whose discount code was entered at checkout. If a customer uses your code, you earn commission even if they did not click your link first. _Example: a customer clicks Creator A's link, then enters Creator B's coupon code. Under this setting, Creator B gets the commission._

**In short:**_if only one creator is involved in the sale, that creator gets paid. The attribution setting is brand-wide and only matters when two different creators are both connected to the same conversion - one via a clicked link, the other via a redeemed code._

# Do I Have to Accept Affiliate Program Terms Before Promoting a Brand on Levanta?

Last updated 4 months ago

**Short Answer: Yes.**

When you apply to join a Seller’s affiliate program on Levanta, you are **required to accept their individual Affiliate Program Agreement** before the partnership becomes active. This is a standard industry practice across affiliate platforms, and it defines the legal and business relationship between you (the Creator) and the Seller (the Brand).

## **What Are Affiliate Program Terms?**

Affiliate Program Terms are the **rules and guidelines** set by each Seller that govern how you may promote their products, what compensation you can expect, and what is and isn't allowed in your promotions.

These may include:

  * **Commission structure** (e.g., CPC, CPA, or hybrid)

  * **Allowed promotional channels** (social, blog, email, etc.)

  * **Branding requirements or restrictions**

  * **Use of coupons or discounts**

  * **Prohibited marketing tactics** (e.g., bidding on brand keywords in search ads)

## **Are All Terms the Same?**

No. While the **majority of Sellers on Levanta use standard Affiliate Program Terms** , some Sellers may upload **custom terms**. These may reflect brand-specific policies or country-specific rules.

You’ll be able to review the terms **at the time of application**. Once accepted, the terms remain binding for the duration of your partnership with that Seller.

**Reminder:** Levanta does **not** mediate or intervene in the terms or enforcement of individual affiliate agreements. The relationship is **directly between the Creator and the Seller**.

## **Where Do I Accept Terms?**

When you apply to a Seller’s program on Levanta, a **terms acceptance checkbox** is shown on the application screen. This is required to proceed.

Once you accept:

  * The Seller can review and approve (or deny) your application

  * If accepted, you will gain access to affiliate links, promotions, and payment tracking for that brand

## **Can I Revisit the Terms Later?**

Yes, you can. Just go to the Brand's page and click on the Terms and Conditions button. You can also download the terms and conditions of your partnered brands.

## **What If I Violate a Seller’s Terms?**

Violating a Seller's terms can lead to:

  * Immediate removal from that Seller’s program

  * Forfeiture of pending commissions

  * Potential banning from the Levanta platform (for repeat or severe violations)

Always ensure that your promotional efforts align with what the Seller has outlined.

# Earn More When Shoppers Check Out with Prime

Last updated 1 month ago

A growing number of the Shopify brands you promote through Levanta now offer Fast, Free Delivery for Prime members directly on their own sites through Amazon's Multichannel Fulfillment (MCF with Prime) and Buy with Prime. When a shopper you referred completes their purchase using their Prime membership at participating brands, you earn more - automatically.

## The Headline: Up to 3% More Commission

When a shopper you drive completes a Shopify purchase using their Prime membership, your commission on that order is augmented by **up to 3%** on top of your standard rate. You don't have to do anything differently to earn it - no separate link, no new tracking setup. If the order qualifies, the boost is applied for you.

This pays out the same way the rest of your earnings do: it's included in your **regular Stripe payments** on your normal Levanta payout schedule. There's no separate Prime payout to chase and no new account to connect. The augmented amount simply shows up in the commission you're already being paid.

## Why You Actually Want to Send Shoppers to Prime-Enabled Pages

This isn't only about the extra 3% on your side - Prime makes the pages you're already promoting convert better, which means more of your clicks turn into paid orders. That's a direct lift to your earnings regardless of the commission boost. The data behind it is strong:

  * **40% of Prime members** said they're more likely to place a first-time purchase with a direct-to-consumer store that features the Amazon Prime logo.

  * Merchants offering Buy with Prime saw, on average, a **20% increase in shopper conversion** and a **16% increase in revenue per shopper**.

  * **50% of U.S. Prime members** said they're more likely to buy again from sites that offer Prime shopping benefits - so the audiences you send convert and come back.

Put simply, a Prime-enabled product page is one of the higher-converting destinations you can link to. Sending traffic there is good for your conversion rate and unlocks the commission boost on top.

## Why did Levanta send a 1099-K instead of a 1099-NEC?

You may have seen news that the IRS has delayed its enforcement of the $600+ threshold for 1099-K and is maintaining the previous 1099-K reporting threshold of $20,000.

We are aware of this IRS threshold change and have worked with 1099 experts to develop our filing approach. Based on the tax filing laws reviewed, we have determined that filing 1099-K for $600 and up is the preferred approach for Levanta Sellers and Creators (as opposed to filing 1099-NEC for those earning $600 and up).

While the IRS has delayed enforcement of the threshold, the law passed by Congress still states that platforms (like Levanta) should be filing 1099-K. The 1099-K law is better suited for Levanta’s role as a platform in the Creator-Seller relationship, and helps us better automate the tax form filing process for our users.

# Why are my Clicks or Sales not Showing Up in Levanta?

Last updated 4 days ago

If you’ve driven traffic or sales today but don’t see them reflected in Levanta, there are a few key reasons why this may be happening.

## **Amazon Data Processing & Reporting Delays**

Amazon Attribution data has a **24-hour delay** , meaning it may take up to a full day before conversions and traffic appear in your Levanta reporting. Additionally, **product reporting can take up to 2 business days** to start displaying results.

## **Click Threshold Requirement**

Amazon requires that each link **receive at least 10 clicks** before traffic and conversion data are unlocked in Levanta. Any sales or activity before hitting this threshold are still tracked in the system, but will not be displayed until the requirement is met.

  * If you’ve just generated a new link, it will need to accumulate **at least 10 clicks** before reporting unlocks.

  * Each time you add source information to a URL, it creates a new, unique link that also needs to meet the **10-click threshold** before data is shown.

  * To expedite the process, **you can click on your link multiple times** to help unlock the data.

## **Troubleshooting Missing Clicks or Sales**

If your clicks are not registering or if you suspect an issue with data tracking, please follow these steps:

  1. Ensure your link has received **at least 10 clicks**.

  2. Wait **24-48 hours** for Amazon Attribution to process the data.

  3. If you still do not see your traffic or conversions reflected, contact [**_cs@levanta.io_**](mailto:cs@levanta.io) with the following details:

     * The ASIN or Item ID, depending on the marketplace.

     * The **Levanta links** you are using.

     * A brief description of the issue you’re experiencing.

## **Walmart and Shopify**

There's no minimum click threshold on Walmart or Shopify, so clicks appear without needing to reach a click count first.

If clicks or sales aren't appearing, check that you're using a link generated in Levanta rather than a standard Walmart URL or a direct link to the brand's store. Levanta Shopify links begin with [lvnta.com/shop/](http://lvnta.com/shop/).

If you've confirmed you're using a Levanta-generated link and data still isn't appearing, email [_cs@levanta.io_](mailto:cs@levanta.io) with the link you used and the approximate date of the click or order.

Our team will investigate further and assist in resolving any potential tracking issues.

**For more information on setting up tracking and optimizing your links, refer to our guide on**[**Generating and Managing Affiliate Links in Levanta**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/7609349103-generating-and-managing-affiliate-links-in-levanta)**.**

# Why Did My Conversion Get Removed?

  * Commissions apply only to:

    * Direct product links.

    * Other variants of the linked product.

    * Other products within the same brand catalog.

  * Commissions **do not** apply to all other products in a shopper’s cart unless they meet the criteria above.

If you believe there was an error in your commission calculation, we recommend reviewing the criteria above to ensure eligibility.

Levanta tracks returns and other scenarios where a conversion is rescinded. # Understanding Team Members, User Roles & Permissions

Last updated 4 months ago

Levanta offers four user roles to help you manage your team's access to your creator account. Each role is designed for different levels of responsibility, from full administrative control to unbiased, content-focused access.

## **The Four User Roles**

### **Admin**

Full access to everything. Admins can manage all aspects of your Levanta account, including payments, invoicing, team settings, reporting, and all creator tools. This role is typically reserved for account owners or senior team members who need complete control.

### **Member**

Access to all reporting, performance data, and creator tools. Members can view commission rates, loyalty bonus targets, and earnings against those bonuses. However, Members cannot access payment information, invoicing, or update team details. This role is ideal for team members who need visibility into performance without financial administration responsibilities.

### **Contributor**

Access to products, brands, messaging, samples, and all creator tools. Contributors can also view commission rates and loyalty bonus targets to make informed decisions about which products to promote. They cannot access reporting, performance data, or earnings information. This role works well for team members focused on content creation and strategic product selection.

### **Editor**

The most focused role, with access limited to products, brands, messaging, and samples. Editors cannot view any monetary information, including commission rates or loyalty bonus targets. This role is designed for team members who help with content and product management but don't need visibility into financial details in any way.

# Creator FAQ's

Last updated 4 days ago

# Frequently asked questions about the Creator side of the Levanta platform.

### **Q: Is Levanta affiliate-only, or can I do paid / flat-fee deals too?**

Levanta supports both! You can earn through affiliate commissions and through paid/flat-fee brand deals. It’s up to you how you work with brands on the platform.

  * Affiliate deals: Earn a commission every time someone purchases through your unique tracking link. There’s no upfront payment. Your earnings are tied directly to sales performance.

  * Paid / flat-fee deals: Some brands on Levanta offer payment for content, regardless of how many sales your post drives. This gives you guaranteed income in addition to (or instead of) commission earnings. Just be sure to fill out your rate card on the platform!

Many creators use a mix of both, and that’s what we recommend too. Paid Placements for guaranteed income and affiliate links layered in for ongoing passive earnings.

### **Q: What is Levanta, and how does it work?**

Levanta is an affiliate marketing platform that connects Amazon, Walmart, and Shopify brands directly with content creators like you. Here’s how it works in three simple steps:

  * A brand lists its products on Levanta and sets a commission rate it’ll pay creators.

  * You browse available deals in your Levanta dashboard, apply to partner with brands that fit your audience, and receive a unique affiliate link for each product.

  * When your audience clicks your link and buys the product, you earn a commission

  * Request samples to create content for your audience.

  * If you have Amazon goals/bonuses, you can still earn towards those goals by utilizing our Creator Connections integration and getting cash Loyalty Bonuses! Please check our knowledge base article for more information about [**Creator Connections & Loyalty Bonuses on Levanta**](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/8897210461-creator-connections-loyalty-bonuses-on-levanta)**.**

Levanta gives brands the ability to offer higher, custom commission rates, which means more earning potential for you.

### **Q: How does the commission structure work? How much can I earn?**

How much commission you earn depends on each brand. Here’s how commissions work across each marketplace:

**Amazon**

  * You earn the commission rate the brand sets on Levanta. Brands typically offer higher rates than Amazon’s native affiliate program (usually 3-5x) specifically to incentivize creators to promote their products.

  * Note: You cannot stack the brand’s Levanta commission with Amazon’s native affiliate rate. You earn one or the other, and the Levanta rate is almost always higher.

**Walmart**

  * Walmart is the best of both worlds. Through Levanta, you can earn both the seller’s commission and Walmart’s own affiliate commission, stacked together on each sale.

**Shopify (Direct-to-Brand Website)**

  * Some brands use Levanta to drive traffic directly to their own website (powered by Shopify). In these cases, the brand sets its own custom commission rate for purchases made through your link.

**Note:** Exact rates vary by brand and marketplace. Always check the deal details in your Levanta dashboard before promoting.

### **Q: What brands are on the platform?**

Levanta works with over 3k brands across a wide range of categories, including but not limited to:

  * Health & wellness

  * Beauty & personal care

  * Home & kitchen

  * Sports & outdoors

  * Tech & electronics accessories

  * Pet products

  * Baby & family

Some notable brands on the platform include Kodak, Summer Fridays, AG1, SunBum, Ourplace, Flavcity, Hero Cosmetics, Kong, and more. The catalog is constantly growing as new brands join.

You can browse all available brand deals directly in your Levanta dashboard after signing up. Deals are searchable by category, commission rate, and product type, so you can find products that genuinely fit your content.

### **Q: What are the deliverables/posting requirements?**

There are no mandatory posting requirements to use Levanta. You are not locked into a content schedule or required to make a specific number of posts to maintain access to affiliate links.

That said, here’s what’s expected:

  * Share your unique Levanta affiliate link in your content (video descriptions, bio links, social posts, blog articles, etc.).

  * Follow FTC guidelines by clearly disclosing your affiliate relationship when promoting products (e.g., “#ad”, “#affiliate”, or “I earn a commission on purchases”).

  * Samples are sent in good faith by brands hoping for promotion. Posting is encouraged but not mandatory. However, creators who consistently request samples without posting may be flagged for abuse.

  * Some individual Paid Placements may have specific requirements or exclusivity terms. Always review the deal terms before accepting.

### **Q: How do I access loyalty bonuses?**

Levanta offers loyalty bonuses as a way to reward top-performing creators and long-term partners. Here’s how to find and unlock them:

  * To join our Creator Connection integration and be able to accept Loyalty Bonuses, email your Amazon Store ID to [cs@levanta.io](mailto:cs@levanta.io).

  * Log in to your Levanta dashboard and navigate to the Partnerships>My Loyalty Program section.

  * Bonuses are available on select brand partnerships and are triggered when you hit specific performance milestones (e.g., a certain number of sales or a revenue threshold within a period).

  * Once a bonus is unlocked, it is automatically added to your next payout.

Not all brands offer loyalty bonuses. In the “My Loyalty Programs" section, toggle to “Brands Offering Bonuses” to see what brands offer them, or when discovering products, filter by “Loyalty Bonus Products”. Be sure to message the brand to ask them to send you a Loyalty Bonus, and make sure to join all the CC campaigns for the Loyalty Bonuses you are sent!

### **Q: How do I request product samples from brands?**

Once your partnership with a Seller is active, you can request product samples directly through Levanta on eligible Amazon and Shopify products (Walmart sampling isn't currently supported). Navigate to **Partnerships > My Products** and look for products that have sampling enabled. Not all products are eligible, as this is determined by the brand. On any sample-enabled product, click **Request Sample** to submit your request. Your shipping information is handled securely through Levanta and shared directly with the fulfillment source (Amazon or the brand's Shopify store), so you won't need to provide it to the brand manually. You'll receive an in-app notification once the brand approves or declines your request.

**Note:** Sample availability is subject to your [_creator tier limits_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/8709430116-understanding-creator-tiers-on-levanta). You can also check our guide on [_How to Request Samples_](https://knowledge.levanta.io/articles/4668077258-how-to-request-samples).

### **Q: Why did my conversion get removed?**

Levanta tracks returns and other scenarios where a conversion is rescinded. The locking period, after which a return no longer affects your commission, varies by marketplace:

  * **Amazon:** 30 days after the end of the month in which the conversion was driven.

  * **Walmart:** follows a longer cycle in line with Walmart's own payout terms.

  * **Shopify:** set by the individual brand (commonly 30, 60, or 90 days).

Once a conversion passes its marketplace's locking window, it's considered final and won't be adjusted further.

### **Q: I know traffic/sales have been driven today, but why are they not showing up in Levanta?**

  * **Amazon:** You'll begin seeing traffic after the first 10 clicks are recorded, and conversions after the first sale. Amazon Attribution data has a 24-hour delay for clicks, and conversions can take up to 48 hours to fully process.

  * **Walmart:** There's no click threshold before your data appears, and by the next business day the sale is usually reflected in your hub.

  * **Shopify:** There's no click threshold, and reporting is close to real-time with no meaningful delay.

### **Q: When do I get paid my commission after a new conversion?**

You’ll get paid 30 days after the end of the month in which the conversion was driven.

Invoices are sent to Brands for conversions you’ve driven within the first few days of the month after a conversion was made. You get paid 30 days after the invoice is sent to the brand. For example, if you drove a conversion on Feb 12th, the brand would be invoiced for that conversion on March 1st. You’ll be paid out on April 1st. Keep in mind that it may take a few business days for the payment to process after it is sent.
